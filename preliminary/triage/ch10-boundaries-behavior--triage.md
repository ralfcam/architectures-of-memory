## 6. Prohibited inferences

- Impulsivity ≠ moral failure
- Immediate compliance ≠ conscience
- Noncompliance ≠ behavior problem
- Toddler hitting ≠ later violence as destiny
- Physical punishment is not a developmental necessity — and using it does not prove a damaged child
- Opposition can reflect autonomy, limited regulation, or communication difficulty
- A parent-training effect does not prove parents caused the original problem
- Do not re-extract sleep, attachment, or screen theory as “discipline”
