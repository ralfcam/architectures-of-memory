# Chapter triage

Chapter-level extraction plans live here. They are **not** evidence cards.

A triage note answers: what files belong to this chapter, what overlaps exist, which claims should be extracted first, and what must not be inferred. Atomic claims, source IDs, and promotion labels belong in `../research/extraction/`.

## Part I sequence

Promote a contiguous arc rather than all 15 dossiers at once.

| Priority | Chapter | Theme | Why it comes first |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | Framing / field guide | Definitions, reader contract, Science–Interpretation–Art |
| 2 | 2 | The First Environment | Earliest substantive territory; overlaps the existing prenatal-stress card |
| 3 | 3 | The Great Transition | Bridges prenatal environment and postnatal regulation |
| 4 | 4 | The Regulatory Relationship | Central relational mechanism |
| 5 | 5 | Becoming a Mind | Perception, attention, learning before language/executive chapters |
| 6 | 7 | The Birth of Language | Dedicated synthesis; connects to the screen-time/language card |

Curated, voice-checked copies now exist for Chapters 1–15. `v0.1` and `v0.2` are tagged on `main`; a v0.3 slice definition lives in `preliminary/releases/v0.3-slice.md`.

## Required sections in a triage note

1. Files and overlaps
2. Developmental window
3. Candidate card families (not the cards themselves)
4. Existing KB overlap
5. Extraction order
6. Prohibited inferences
7. Promotion blockers

## Status vocabulary

Same as `MANIFEST.md`: `unreviewed`, `triage-ready`, `extracted`, `audited`, `promoted`.
