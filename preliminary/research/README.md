# Preliminary Research Notes

This subdirectory holds raw literature notes, annotated bibliographies, and work-in-progress evidence syntheses that have not yet been formalized into OKF evidence cards.

## Purpose

- Capture ideas and sources quickly without worrying about frontmatter or OKF structure.
- Serve as a scratchpad for triangulating evidence before writing a formal evidence card.

## When Ready

Once a note is sufficiently developed:
1. Extract atomic claims into `extraction/` (see that folder's README). Do not turn a whole dossier into one card.
2. Convert audited claims into OKF evidence cards in `../../evidence-cards/` (with `type: Evidence Card`, `sources`, `status`, etc.).
3. Add or update sources in `../../SOURCES.md` in the same commit.
4. Log the promotion in `../../CHANGELOG.md`.

Leave original uploads in place. Curated copies use the naming convention in `../README.md`.

You can delete or archive the preliminary note after promotion, or keep it as a historical trail.
