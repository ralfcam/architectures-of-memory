## 3. Prohibited inferences

- The first word is not the beginning of language.
- A laboratory segmentation result is not household vocabulary.
- Conversational-turn counts do not cause later IQ.
- Hart & Risley’s thirty million is not a causal metric of parental quality.
- Dual-language exposure is not confusion.
- Perceptual narrowing is not a sealed deadline for a second language.
- Screens are not a unitary harm.
