### CH13-C02 — Income and cash transfers

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Claim | Poverty is associated with disadvantage through multiple pathways. A defined monthly cash transfer (Baby’s First Years) changed infant EEG power without preregistered age-four cognitive gains. |
| Sources | Troller-Renfree et al. 2022 *PNAS* DOI 10.1073/pnas.2115649119; Noble et al. 2025 NBER w33844 DOI 10.3386/w33844. |
| Label | `promote` — card `evidence-cards/income-cash-transfer-early-development.md` |
| Grade | Moderate gradient; Low-to-Moderate cash-as-cognition. |

### CH13-C03 / C13-C08 — Housing and neighborhood mobility

| Field | Content |
|---|---|

| Label | `promote` — card `evidence-cards/neighborhood-mobility-age-dependent.md` |
