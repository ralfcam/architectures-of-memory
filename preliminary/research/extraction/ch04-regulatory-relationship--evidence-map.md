## 4. Prohibited inferences (still in force)

- The Strange Situation is not a personality test.
- Sensitivity is not a script for destiny.
- Responding to crying is not automatically “spoiling”; waiting is not automatically “teaching independence.”
- Severe institutional deprivation is not ordinary parenting variation.
- Nurturing care is an ecology, not a maternal purity test.
