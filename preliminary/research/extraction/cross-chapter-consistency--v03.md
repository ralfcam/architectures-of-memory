# Cross-chapter consistency — regulation, causation, risk, resilience, attachment, institutions

Date: 2026-09-16. After `v0.3` (`21d1a0d`). This note is a manuscript-level audit of six load-bearing terms across curated Chapters 1–15. It does not rewrite sleep, attachment-intervention, or screen-time *theory*. **No card to `stable`.**

Related: [second-pass audit](draft-cards--second-pass-audit.md) · [manuscript revision pass](manuscript-revision-pass--v03.md)

## Method

Read every curated `chapters/0N-*.md` for those six terms. A term is *coherent* when (a) the chapter that owns the identification keeps it, (b) later chapters reconcile rather than re-estimate, and (c) forbidden collapses are restated where the collapse typically returns.

## Findings

| Term | Owning identification | How later chapters use it | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Attachment** | Ch. 4: co-regulation is bidirectional and context-sensitive; sensitivity RCTs move sensitivity more than security; BEIP is not ordinary care. | Ch. 8: stranger wariness ≠ insecure attachment; multiple attachments occur, network size is not a dose. Ch. 9: still-face ≠ attachment classification. Ch. 10/12/13/15: “sleep and attachment-intervention theory are not restated.” | Coherent. The popular pipeline (responsive parent → secure attachment → later success) is refused in Ch. 4 and not restored. |
| **Regulation** | Ch. 4: *relational* co-regulation, not a purity test. Feeding-as-regulation is cue-based, not a growth program. | Ch. 9: emotion/still-face/temperament; Ch. 10: compliance and routines; Ch. 11: EF/inhibition as task families; Ch. 13: ecology can look like “parenting quality” on a tape. | Coherent *if* the object is named. Residual risk: the English word “regulation” still covers feeding, emotion, EF, and ecology. This pass does not collapse them. Ch. 11 still does not steal Ch. 10’s “no.” |
| **Causation** | CONTRIBUTING: association / prediction / contribution / causation / prevention / determinism. GI attenuation is a finding (Ch. 1–2 prenatal stress; Watts delay; breastfeeding IQ). | Ch. 7: turns *predict*, do not cause. Ch. 13: cash EEG vs age-four nulls held together. Ch. 14: association is not an intervention target; efficacy ≠ effectiveness ≠ scale. | Coherent. GI language is strongest in Ch. 1–2 and on the Watts card; first-pass observational cards still say “not classified.” That is a catalog gap, not a manuscript contradiction. |
| **Risk** | Ch. 3: preterm *group* SMD. Ch. 9: inhibition as *group* social-anxiety risk. | Ch. 12: “risky play” is a school-age literature, not a 0–3 law (`outdoor-risky-play-not-0-3-law.md`). Ch. 1: folate *risk reduction* ≠ child optimization. | Coherent after naming the outdoor card. Group-risk ≠ individual forecast is already the Ch. 3/9 rule; Ch. 12’s “risk” is an exposure label, not a temperament. |
| **Resilience** | Ch. 15: Masten ordinary adaptive systems; High existence; Moderate mechanism; Low costless. Not a parental virtue. | Ch. 1 and Ch. 14 use “resilience” only as something a supplement or program must *not* be taken to manufacture. | Coherent. This pass adds an explicit Ch. 14 S/I/A pointer to the Ch. 15 grade so the word cannot be read as a technique. |
| **Institutional context** | Ch. 13: income/cash, MTO age-at-move, leave package, food insecurity as material conditions. BEIP is the upper bound, not housing or childcare. | Ch. 8: group care ≠ deprivation. Ch. 4/15: parenting is a proximal process inside rent, hours, and institutions. | Coherent. This pass names leave and food cards in Ch. 13 so those objects are no longer “held as unregistered.” |

## Remaining tensions (not silently papered over)

1. **Chapter 2 length.** Curated Ch. 2 is ~2,358 words (slightly under Appendix B). Not padded in this pass.
2. **Chapter 14 form.** Numbered intervention architecture rather than the 10-step rhythm. This pass adds a visible Science / Interpretation / Art beat; it does not convert Ch. 14 into a 10-step essay.
3. **Held leftovers that are still correctly held:** neonatal perception and mode of delivery (Ch. 3); routines/modeling (Ch. 10); dedicated WM/shifting and private speech (Ch. 11); guided-play efficacy and ToM-transfer (Ch. 12).
4. **“Regulation” as a family resemblance.** A future pass could add a one-line crosswalk in Ch. 11 (“this is EF, not Ch. 4’s co-regulation”) — already present in spirit; not duplicated here as a new theory.

## Prohibited inferences this note itself refuses

- Consistency ≠ every chapter using the same sentence.
- Naming a remaining-domain card ≠ promoting it to `stable`.
- Ordinary resilience ≠ adversity is harmless.
- Institutional packages ≠ parental failure.
