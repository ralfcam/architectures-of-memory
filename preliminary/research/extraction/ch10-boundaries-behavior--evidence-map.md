| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Claim | Behavioral organization increases across toddlerhood and remains externally supported. Frequent impulsive action is common and is not itself a moral failure. |
| Label | **Reconcile** with `inhibitory-control-toddler.md` (age trend) plus interpretive chapter architecture. |
| Grade | Moderate-to-High as population description. |

### CH10-C03 — Committed vs situational compliance

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Claim | Willing, self-regulated cooperation is not the same as pressure-dependent obedience; the former is more closely linked to later internalization in Kochanska’s program. Immediate compliance is not conscience. |
| Sources | Kochanska 1995 *Child Development* DOI 10.2307/1131908. |
| Label | `promote` — card `evidence-cards/committed-compliance-not-obedience.md` |
| Grade | Moderate distinction; Low technique. |

|---|---|

## 3. Prohibited inferences

- Impulsivity ≠ moral failure
- Compliance ≠ conscience
- Hitting ≠ destiny
- Punishment necessity is not supported
- Intervention success ≠ parents caused the problem
