WHO Nurturing Care is a framework synthesis (health, nutrition, security/safety, responsive caregiving, early learning). It is used here to bound “responsive care” as ecological, not as a causal estimate of ordinary parent differences.

Feldman 2012 is a theoretical/psychophysiological model of bio-behavioral synchrony. It supports the *existence* of coupled dynamics; it does not license a single synchrony score as developmental benefit.

BEIP is an RCT of foster care versus continued institutional care among already-institutionalized abandoned children in Bucharest. Generalization to ordinary parenting, daycare, or sleep training is prohibited.
