## Why this is not Chapter 2 again

Chapter 2 already extracted pregnancy-as-mediated-environment (timing, placenta, neural construction, folate, iodine, alcohol, tobacco, mixtures, growth). Preconception is the slice **before a recognized pregnancy**: gametes, age, nutrients the embryo will need in the first weeks, and the social conditions that shape those things.

C02-P1 opens as a rewrite of “The First Environment.” Use it to recover named preconception studies; do not double-extract C02-D1’s pregnancy cards.
