### CH11-C01 / C11-C02 — EF as a family of related control processes

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Claim | Inhibitory control, working-memory updating, and shifting share variance and are separable; toddler forms are precursors, not miniature adult EF. EF is not intelligence or obedience. |
| Sources | Miyake et al. 2000 *Cognitive Psychology* DOI 10.1006/cogp.1999.0734; Garon, Bryson & Smith 2008 *Psychological Bulletin* DOI 10.1037/0033-2909.134.1.31. |
| Label | `promote` — card `evidence-cards/executive-function-unity-diversity.md` |
| Grade | Moderate-to-High construct; Moderate toddler factor structure. |

### CH11-C03 — Inhibitory control improves and remains context-bound

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Claim | Response suppression becomes more reliable across toddlerhood/preschool under particular cues and supports. Symbolic distancing can improve performance. Failure to inhibit is not character. |
| Sources | Garon 2008; Carlson 2005 *Developmental Neuropsychology* DOI 10.1207/s15326942dn2802_3; Carlson, Davis & Leach 2005 *Psychological Science* DOI 10.1111/j.1467-9280.2005.01583.x. |
| Label | `promote` — card `evidence-cards/inhibitory-control-toddler.md` |
| Grade | Moderate-to-High age trend; Moderate for any single task. |
