Looking-time, dual-language, babbling, Lillard, and deferred imitation remain `draft`.

A card may move to `stable` only after all five rules in `catalog-wide-replication-attenuation.md`:

1. Sources remain resolvable.
2. No retraction or expression-of-concern for the primary ID.
3. Replication or GI attenuation has been classified where that design class exists.
4. In-body grade still matches YAML `confidence`.
5. “What This Does Not Mean” still matches the identification.

Grey-literature IDs without a journal DOI (`nichd-seccyd-2006`, `who-anc-2016`, `who-nurturing-care-2018`, `kramer-1987-lbw`) now have persistent URLs or PMID (`SOURCES.md`). They should not be load-bearing primary IDs for a first `stable` card. `hiscock-2016` remains retired. `who-anc-2016` was dropped from the folate card’s `sources:` array (framework only).
