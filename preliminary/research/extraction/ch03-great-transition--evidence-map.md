## 3. Prohibited inferences (from triage; still in force)

- Birth is not the beginning of the organism.
- Birth is not a reset of prenatal development.
- A difficult transition is not a personality forecast.
- Caregivers participate in regulation; they do not replace the placenta as an organ.
- Mode of delivery is not character.
- SSC does not guarantee bonding, breastfeeding, or later cognition.
