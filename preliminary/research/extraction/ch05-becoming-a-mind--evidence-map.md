## 3. Prohibited inferences (still in force)

- A look is not a belief.
- Attention is not a moral trait.
- Statistical learning is not a complete theory of grammar or mind.
- Contingency is not adult causal theory.
- Object permanence is not a single staircase.
- Joint attention is not a language guarantee or a purity test.
- More stimulation is not necessarily better.
