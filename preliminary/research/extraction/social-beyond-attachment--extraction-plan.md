## What “beyond attachment” means here

Attachment remains a Chapter 4 object (`infant-caregiver-coregulation`, `responsive-caregiving-attachment-interventions`, BEIP). This domain is extrafamilial and extra-dyadic: peers, stranger wariness, social referencing, expanding caregiver networks, group care as a social environment, siblings/kin as distinct functions.
