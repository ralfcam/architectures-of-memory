## 2. Candidate claims

Labels: `promote` · `needs-source-audit` · `interpretive-only` · `exclude`.

### CH01-C01 — Parental biology before conception is already a developmental environment

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Claim | Eggs, sperm, nutrients, toxins, age, illness, and the social conditions that shape those things operate before a positive test; “starts before birth” is a statement about conditions, not a completed biography. |
| Sources | WHO preconception-care frame (named in C01-D1); folate card as the calibrated case. |
| Label | `interpretive-only` as a standalone card. Empirical pieces live on timing and folate cards. |
| Grade | High as biology of sequence; Very Low as parental control. |

### CH01-C02 — Timing windows before implantation / organogenesis are process-specific

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Claim | Preimplantation life, gastrulation, and organogenesis are different jobs; an exposure during specification is not the same exposure after the structure exists. Neural-tube closure is a periconceptional clock. |
| Sources | Ujházy et al. 2012 (`ujhazy-2012-teratology`). |
| Label | `promote` already — card `prenatal-developmental-timing.md`. Reconcile only. |
| Grade | High that pregnancy is not one nine-month window; Moderate for Wilson’s process-specific principle. |

### CH01-C03 — Epigenetic marking without determinism

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Claim | Chemical marks can change how DNA is used during gametogenesis and early embryonic life; that process is real and is not a script for a child’s later character written by ordinary parental mood or diet. |
| Sources | C01-D1 register: paternal/epigenetic pathways Low-to-Moderate in humans; animal mechanism ≠ human childhood. |
| Label | `interpretive-only` for this chapter. Do not extract a generic “epigenetics of parenting” card. |
| Grade | Moderate that the process class exists; Low for ordinary-range human causal stories; Very Low as a parental sentence. |

### CH01-C04 — Periconceptional folate prevents a defined embryonic defect

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Claim | Daily periconceptional folic acid reduces neural-tube-defect-affected pregnancy (Cochrane RR 0.31, 0.17–0.58; 6,708 births). Not other anomalies, intelligence, or a better human being. |
| Sources | De-Regil 2015; MRC 1991. |
| Label | `promote` already — card `prenatal-folate-ntd.md`. Reconcile only. |
| Grade | High for NTD; Low for later cognition. |

### CH01-C05 — Typical-range prenatal stress/anxiety associations largely attenuate under genetically informed designs

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Claim | Observational prenatal anxiety/stress associations with later internalizing often shrink or disappear in sibling, children-of-twins, IVF/adoption, and related designs. Disaster/clinical trauma is not this claim. |
| Sources | MoBa triangulation; MCS sibling; JAACAP GI meta; children-of-twins. |
| Label | `promote` already — card `prenatal-maternal-stress-anxiety.md`. Reconcile only. |
| Grade | Low-to-Moderate (moderate that typical-range associations are largely confounded). |

### CH01-C06 — Paternal age and de novo mutation rate (narrow genetic observation)

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Claim | De novo mutations in offspring increase with father’s age (Kong et al. 2012: on the order of two additional mutations per year). This is a mutational relationship, not a psychiatric or cognitive forecast in ordinary ranges. |
| Sources | Kong et al. 2012 *Nature* DOI 10.1038/nature11396. |
| Label | `promote` — card `evidence-cards/paternal-age-de-novo-mutations.md` |
| Grade | High for the mutational relationship; Low that ordinary-range paternal age determines a child’s psychiatric or cognitive future. |

### CH01-C07 — Prenatal neural construction is unfinished at birth

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Claim | Construction proceeds through overlapping proliferation, migration, and transient embryonic zones; unfinished cortex is not a forecast of intelligence or personality. |
| Sources | Bystron, Blakemore & Rakic 2008. |
| Label | `promote` already — card `prenatal-neural-construction.md`. Reconcile only. |
| Grade | High for structural sequence; Low for personality forecast. |

## 3. Prohibited inferences

From the dossier’s own bounded claim, still in force:

- Biological relevance ≠ parental control
- Association ≠ causation
- Early difference ≠ persistent effect
- Modifiability ≠ individual responsibility
- Family action ≠ substitute for institutional obligation
- Epigenetic marking ≠ a completed character
- Paternal-age mutation rate ≠ a fertility instruction
- Unfinished cortex ≠ intelligence forecast

Do not extract a generic “early development matters” card.
