## Abstract

Human development has relevant antecedents before birth, and the transition to parenthood changes the world into which a child is born. That claim is High as biology and Very Low as a license to read a future from a fetus or to treat parents as sole engineers of that future. Periconceptional folic acid substantially reduces neural-tube-defect risk; it does not manufacture intelligence. Timing, unfinished cortex, paternal-age mutation rates, and typical-range prenatal stress are separate graded claims. Science, Interpretation, and Art must not collapse. Biological relevance is not parental control.

## What this chapter does not license

- Biological relevance is not parental control.
- Association is not causation.
- An early difference is not a persistent effect.
- Modifiability is not individual responsibility.
- Family action is not a substitute for institutional obligation.

High on a structural embryonic outcome does not travel. Folate’s High grade is for neural-tube-defect risk. It is Low for later cognition.
