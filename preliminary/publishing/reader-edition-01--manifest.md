## Change boundary

This edition freezes manuscript presentation, not the evidence catalog. Reader feedback may motivate a bounded editorial issue. It cannot change a grade, validate a causal claim, promote an evidence card, or become a recommendation by agreement. Factual concerns must return to the normal evidence-card and extraction workflow before chapter prose changes.

The frozen catalog remains 68 cards: 7 narrowly `stable`, 61 `draft`; 114 sources. This artifact is not `v0.4`, a knowledge-base release, a public preprint, or a finished publishing master.
