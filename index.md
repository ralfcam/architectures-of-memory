# Parenthood: The Atlas

A longitudinal evidence atlas of developmental science, spanning approximately one year before conception through age 3. This bundle conforms to the Open Knowledge Format (OKF) v0.2.

# Knowledge

* [Evidence Cards](evidence-cards/index.md) - Preliminary and audited evidence cards synthesizing developmental research, one concept per topic.
* [Master Source Catalog](SOURCES.md) - Deduplicated bibliography of every source cited across the evidence cards, with verification status and back-references.

# Documentation

* [README](README.md) - Project mission, core framework, and governing rules.
* [CONTRIBUTING](CONTRIBUTING.md) - Evidence-evaluation methodology: identification strength, certainty grading, bias/confounding rules.
* [CHANGELOG](CHANGELOG.md) - Human-facing narrative version history of the knowledge base.
