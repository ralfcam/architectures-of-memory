## Why not the 8-chapter seed

The seed list collapsed framing, birth, extrafamilial ecology, intervention, and the age-3 waypoint, and numbered *The First Environment* as Chapter 1. That numbering collides with the written book. Keep `COMPOSITION.md` examples as voice/grammar, not as the TOC.

## Promotion rule

A chapter moves into `chapters/` only after linked evidence cards are audited and a review note exists in `preliminary/drafts-chapters/reviews/`. Do not overwrite archival drafts; write a curated `chNN-…--draft-NN.md` copy.
