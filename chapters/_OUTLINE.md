# Chapter Outline (Seed)

This is a provisional table of contents using the natural-history metaphor. Each chapter will follow the 10-step rhythm in `COMPOSITION.md` Appendix B.

## Provisional Chapters

1. **The First Environment** — Prenatal and perinatal exposures; the womb as a developmental niche.
2. **The Regulatory Relationship** — Co-regulation, sleep, feeding, and the birth of self-control.
3. **Becoming a Mind** — Perception, attention, and the emergence of executive functions.
4. **The Birth of Language** — From babbling to grammar; how children learn words and rules.
5. **The Emotional Revolution** — Attachment, tantrums, and the development of emotional understanding.
6. **The Developing Executive** — Inhibition, planning, and the slow construction of self-regulation.
7. **Play and Imagination** — How children invent worlds and test boundaries.
8. **Morality and Rules** — From rule-following to rule-making; the origins of fairness.

Each chapter should:
- Open with an anomaly (see `style/anomaly-bank.md`).
- Map boundaries between systems (e.g., attachment ↔ regulation, language ↔ executive function).
- Explicitly mark uncertainty (see `CONTRIBUTING.md` certainty grades).
- Return to the child at the end.
