# Evidence Cards Index

Sixty-three evidence cards. **Three** physiology/lab cards are `status: stable` for a *narrow* claim only (`prenatal-folate-ntd.md`, `infant-statistical-speech-segmentation.md`, `neonatal-cardiopulmonary-transition.md`; see `preliminary/research/extraction/stable-candidates--replication-note.md`). The other sixty remain **status: draft** and require deeper audit before being treated as settled entries. See `CONTRIBUTING.md` for the methodology each card follows, `SOURCES.md` for the full bibliography, and [`SPEC.md`](../SPEC.md) for this bundle's OKF v0.2 pointer (canonical spec is external).

# Evidence Cards

* [Prenatal Developmental Timing](prenatal-developmental-timing.md) - Process-specific windows; pregnancy is not a single exposure period.
* [The Placenta Mediates Fetal Dose](placenta-as-mediator.md) - Maternal exposure is not fetal dose; 11β-HSD2 is a real, incomplete glucocorticoid buffer.
* [Prenatal Neural Construction](prenatal-neural-construction.md) - Human cortex is unfinished at birth; structure is not a personality forecast.
* [Fetal Autonomic Maturation](fetal-autonomic-maturation.md) - Fetal HRV indexes present maturation, not temperament destiny.
* [Prenatal Maternal Stress and Anxiety](prenatal-maternal-stress-anxiety.md) - Observational prenatal stress/anxiety associations largely attenuate under genetically informed designs.
* [Prenatal Auditory Discrimination and Newborn Preference](prenatal-auditory-learning.md) - Late-pregnancy speech exposure can shape newborn preference; not lasting cognitive advantage.
* [Periconceptional Folic Acid and Neural-Tube Defects](prenatal-folate-ntd.md) - **`stable`** (narrow NTD claim): RCTs/Cochrane + Czeizel 1992; Low other anomalies / IQ.
* [Prenatal Iodine and Maternal T4](prenatal-iodine-thyroid.md) - Severe early iodine deficiency can injure the developing brain; mild replete-population claims are weaker.
* [Prenatal Alcohol and FASD](prenatal-alcohol-fasd.md) - Causal teratology continuum; detected exposure is not a diagnosis.
* [Maternal Tobacco and Fetal Growth](prenatal-tobacco-birthweight.md) - Dose-related birthweight/preterm associations; not an IQ card.
* [Prenatal Environmental Mixtures](prenatal-environmental-mixtures.md) - Selected phthalate–placenta associations; not proven permanent child injury.
* [Fetal Growth Is Not Destiny](fetal-growth-not-destiny.md) - INTERGROWTH standards measure size; percentile is not character.
* [Neonatal Cardiopulmonary Transition](neonatal-cardiopulmonary-transition.md) - **`stable`** (narrow sequence): birth reorganizes gas exchange and circulation; a difficult transition is not a personality forecast.
* [Early Skin-to-Skin Contact](skin-to-skin-immediate-physiology.md) - Healthy-newborn SSC: moderate for breastfeeding at 1–4 months; low for infant physiology and character.
* [Infant–Caregiver Co-Regulation](infant-caregiver-coregulation.md) - Bidirectional, context-sensitive regulation; nurturing care is an ecology, not a purity test.
* [Severe Institutional Deprivation](severe-institutional-deprivation.md) - BEIP: family placement after severe deprivation is not ordinary parenting.
* [Infant Contingency Learning](infant-contingency-learning.md) - Mobile paradigm: High for action–outcome learning under control; Low for transfer.
* [Looking Time as Measurement](looking-time-as-measurement.md) - A look indexes discrimination or attention; it is not a window into a rich concept.
* [Infant Attention as a Gatekeeper](infant-attention-as-gatekeeper.md) - Attention is a family of processes, not a trait or a parenting score.
* [Object Permanence Is Not a Single Staircase](object-permanence-looking-vs-search.md) - Looking-time sensitivity to occlusion is not the same as search, and not a finished object concept.
* [Joint Attention as Shared Reference](joint-attention-shared-reference.md) - Shared attention is a construct, not a language guarantee.
* [Infant Perceptual Narrowing](infant-perceptual-narrowing.md) - First-year phonetic attunement; High in the classic lab; Low as a sealed deadline.
* [Infant Statistical Speech Segmentation](infant-statistical-speech-segmentation.md) - **`stable`** (narrow lab claim): Saffran 1996 / Aslin 1998; Low for household transfer.
* [Conversational Turns and Later Language](conversational-turns-language.md) - Gilkerson 2018: Moderate prediction from turns, not word counts; Low causation.
* [Pointing as Shared Reference](infant-pointing-as-shared-reference.md) - Colonnesi 2010: Moderate that pointing predicts language; Low that baby-sign products accelerate typical speech.
* [Babbling as Exploration](infant-babbling-as-exploration.md) - Oller 1999: High that canonical babbling is a real stage; Low as an IQ or parenting verdict.
* [Paternal Age and De Novo Mutations](paternal-age-de-novo-mutations.md) - Kong 2012: High for mutation rate with father's age; Low as a cognitive/psychiatric forecast.
* [Infant Social Referencing](infant-social-referencing.md) - Sorce 1985 / Walden 1988: High for laboratory referencing under uncertainty; Low as an attachment verdict.
* [Multiple Caregiver Attachments](infant-multiple-caregiver-attachments.md) - Dagan 2018/2021: Moderate that multiple attachments occur; Low that network size is a dose.
* [Toddler Peer Interaction](toddler-peer-interaction.md) - Brownell 2006: Moderate that peer contexts create coordination demands; Low that hours cause later character.
* [Embodied Perception–Action](embodied-perception-action.md) - Adolph & Hoch 2019: Moderate framework; Low that earlier milestones cause later cognition.
* [Exploration as Sampling](exploration-as-sampling.md) - Stahl & Feigenson 2015: Moderate that surprise reorganizes exploration; Low transfer.
* [Early Physical Reasoning](infant-physical-reasoning.md) - Baillargeon 1987 / Stahl 2015: Moderate VOE; Low complete physics.
* [Deferred Imitation](deferred-imitation.md) - Meltzoff 1988: High for 14-month one-week delay; Low neonate/curriculum.
* [Parent-Implemented Language Interventions](parent-implemented-language.md) - Heidlage 2020: Moderate expressive vocabulary in 25 RCTs; Low universal typical-development IQ.
* [Still-Face Disruption of Expected Contingency](infant-still-face-paradigm.md) - Tronick 1978 / Mesman 2009: High laboratory procedure effect; Low as attachment verdict. Co-regulation card reconciled.
* [Temperament as Reactivity and Control](temperament-reactivity-not-destiny.md) - Gartstein & Rothbart 2003: Moderate dimensional differences; Low destiny.
* [Behavioral Inhibition as Anxiety Risk](behavioral-inhibition-anxiety-risk.md) - Clauss & Blackford 2012: Moderate group social-anxiety risk; Low individual forecast.
* [Executive Function as Unity and Diversity](executive-function-unity-diversity.md) - Miyake 2000 / Garon 2008: Moderate-to-High construct; not intelligence or character. Watts delay card reconciled.
* [Toddler Inhibitory Control](inhibitory-control-toddler.md) - Garon / Carlson: Moderate-to-High age trend; Low marshmallow destiny.
* [Symbolic and Pretend Play Emergence](symbolic-pretend-play-emergence.md) - Belsky & Most 1981: Moderate-to-High second-year emergence; not a diagnostic cutoff.
* [Educational Claims About Pretend Play](pretend-play-educational-claims.md) - Lillard 2013: High that unique-cause educational claims exceed the evidence.
* [Fathers, Kin, and Siblings](father-kin-sibling-heterogeneous-network.md) - McWayne 2013 / Dagan 2018: Moderate association; Low unique cause. More caregivers ≠ better.
* [Committed Compliance Is Not Obedience](committed-compliance-not-obedience.md) - Kochanska 1995: Moderate distinction; Low technique. Immediate compliance ≠ conscience.
* [Toddler Physical Aggression Trajectories](toddler-physical-aggression-trajectories.md) - Tremblay 2004: Moderate-to-High peak-and-decline; Low violence forecast.
* [Physical Punishment Is Not Necessary](physical-punishment-not-necessary.md) - Gershoff 2016: Moderate-to-High not-necessary; not every instance = damaged child.
* [Income Support Is Not a Cognitive Guarantee](income-cash-transfer-early-development.md) - Baby’s First Years: infant EEG difference; age-four cognitive nulls.
* [Neighborhood Moves Are Age-Dependent](neighborhood-mobility-age-dependent.md) - Chetty 2016 MTO: Moderate later earnings; Low-to-Moderate 0–3 measures.
* [Early Rank-Order Continuity Is Modest](rank-order-continuity-not-destiny.md) - Roberts & DelVecchio 2000: childhood consistency about *r* = .31; not adult personality.
* [Childhood Self-Control Gradient](childhood-self-control-gradient.md) - Moffitt 2011 Dunedin: Moderate group gradient; Low individual forecast. Watts delay reconciled.
* [Ordinary Resilience](ordinary-resilience-adaptive-systems.md) - Masten 2001: High that resilience is often ordinary systems; adversity is not harmless.
* [Two Languages Do Not Confuse](dual-language-does-not-confuse.md) - NAP 2017: High that dual exposure as such does not confuse; Low automatic cognitive bonus.
* [Infant Crying as a Signal](infant-crying-as-signal.md) - Graded distress signal; not a one-to-one code for hunger, pain, or parental error.
* [Suck–Swallow–Breathe Coordination](suck-swallow-breathe-coordination.md) - Oral feeding is maturational coordination; feeding method is not parental worth.
* [Responsive Feeding as Regulation](responsive-feeding-as-regulation.md) - Cue-based bidirectional feeding; not a weight or IQ program.
* [Preterm Birth and Group Cognitive Risk](preterm-cognition-group-risk.md) - 2024 meta-analysis: group SMD about −0.57; not an individual sentence.
* [Breastfeeding and Cognitive Development](breastfeeding-cognitive-development.md) - PROBIT RCT and sibling comparisons show a small, fading, verbal-concentrated effect.
* [Early Childcare Quality and Development](early-childcare-quality.md) - NICHD SECCYD cohort: modest quality-cognition links; parenting predicts more.
* [Screen Time and Language Development](screen-time-language-development.md) - Weak-to-moderate observational associations, decomposed by content/co-use/context. Madigan 2020 JAMA vs Jing 2023 *Child Development* IDs repaired.
* [Responsive Caregiving and Attachment Interventions](responsive-caregiving-attachment-interventions.md) - Short sensitivity RCTs move sensitivity more than attachment security.
* [Infant Sleep Training](infant-sleep-training.md) - RCTs and cohorts find no attachment/behavioral harm through age 6; `hiscock-2016` listing retired.
* [Self-Regulation and Delay of Gratification](self-regulation-delay-of-gratification.md) - 2018 NICHD replication shows the original predictor claim was mostly confounded.
* [Preschool / Early Childhood Education](preschool-early-education-rct.md) - Genuine long-run RCT evidence from small, high-fidelity 1960s-70s programs.
