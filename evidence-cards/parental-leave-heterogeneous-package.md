# Parental Leave Is a Heterogeneous Package, Not a Unitary Exposure

> Domain: ecology / employment and leave
> Last updated: 2026-09-16
> Status: Source-audited first pass (CH13-C04/C13-C05 remainder). Income-cash, childcare, and MTO cards are neighbors, not rewritten.

## What We Know

Carneiro, Løken & Salvanes (2015) studied a Norwegian maternity-leave reform that extended paid leave. Children whose mothers were eligible had better long-run education and earnings outcomes than comparison cohorts. The object is a defined leave *package* in one institutional setting, not “employment” as a single exposure.

## How Confident We Are

**Grade**: Moderate that this reform is associated with better later socioeconomic outcomes in this design. **Low-to-Moderate** for direct effects on 0–3 developmental measures. **Low** that every leave or every job has the same child effect.

## Evidence and Causal Assessment

Natural-experiment / reform design, not an RCT of toddler cognition. Long-run education and earnings are not infant test scores. Adjacent: `income-cash-transfer-early-development.md` is a different lever (cash without mandated time). Childcare (`early-childcare-quality.md`) is what leave sometimes replaces. MTO is housing mobility, not leave. Sleep and attachment-intervention theory are not restated.

## Effect Size and Persistence

Long-run education and earnings effects are the identification. Near-term infant scores are not this paper’s object. Persistence here is the adult outcome, not a toddler battery.

## Bias, Heterogeneity, and Confounding

GI attenuation: not classified. Eligibility, wage replacement, duration, and which parent stays home vary across countries. A night shift and a salaried leave are not one exposure. Selection into take-up remains a threat where leave is not universal.

## What Remains Uncertain

How much of the reform effect is time with a familiar caregiver, income smoothing, or reduced employment stress; how findings travel to unpaid or short leaves.

## What This Does Not Mean

Employment is not a unitary harm. Leave is not a guarantee of infant cognition. A missing leave week is not a character defect, and a long leave is not a proven IQ program.

## What Action Is Reasonably Justified

Treat leave as an institutional package. Families cannot individually legislate eligibility. A universal “stay home for cognition” script does not follow.
