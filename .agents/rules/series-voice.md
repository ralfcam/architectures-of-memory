# Series Voice (*Architectures of Memory*)

Full reference: [`series/architectures-of-memory/series-bible.md`](../../series/architectures-of-memory/series-bible.md) and [`COMPOSITION.md`](../../COMPOSITION.md).

- **Tone:** Analytic, historically grounded; name people, papers, dates. No triumphalism, no anthropomorphic AI clichés ([`style/forbidden-phrases.md`](../../style/forbidden-phrases.md)).
- **Three lenses:** **Technical** (mechanism) → **Organizational** (institutions, GRC, handoffs) → **Philosophical** (persons, concepts). Rotate per chapter outline.
- **Metaphors (max one per section):** gradient as **debt**; gate as **judgment**; attention as **valuation**; context as **horizon**; forgetting as **design**.
- **Glossary touchstones:** vanishing gradient; RNN; LSTM / CEC; attention; Transformer; context window; parametric memory; RAG; machine unlearning; right to be forgotten.
- **Spine:** Book 1 = proof of failure; Book 2 = selective forgetting; Book 3 = retrieval vs memory; Book 4 = scale / opacity; Book 5 = governance and ethics of forgetting. Hochreiter 1991 thesis bookends Books 1 and 5.
