# Anomaly Bank (Chapter-Opening Hooks)

Use these (or variants) to open chapters. Each is a surprising observation that contradicts naive expectations.

1. A newborn recognizes a voice heard before birth.
2. A toddler knowingly violates a rule.
3. A child learns a word after a single exposure.
4. A baby cries more intensely when the parent returns.
5. An infant prefers a stranger's face that resembles their caregiver's.
6. A preschooler invents a rule during play and enforces it on adults.
7. A child who hears two languages from birth does not confuse them.
8. A toddler comforts a crying peer before being taught to do so.
9. A child's tantrum de-escalates when the adult regulates first.
10. A preschooler asks "why?" not to learn facts but to test explanations.

Add new anomalies here as they emerge during research or drafting.
