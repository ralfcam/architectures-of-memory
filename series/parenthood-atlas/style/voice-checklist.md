# Voice Checklist (Pre-Commit Review)

Before committing any chapter draft, verify:

## Calm
- [ ] No urgency markers ("must," "immediately," "critical window closing").
- [ ] No alarmism ("ruins," "destroys," "irreversibly harms").
- [ ] No optimization culture ("optimal," "maximize," "perfect").

## Curious
- [ ] Chapter opens with a question or anomaly, not a conclusion.
- [ ] Language is investigative ("we examine," "this raises the question") not instructional ("you should").

## Empirically Disciplined
- [ ] Every causal claim has a visible evidence grade (High/Moderate/Low/Very Low).
- [ ] Uncertainty is explicitly acknowledged where evidence is Low/Very Low.
- [ ] No deterministic phrasing ("X causes Y forever").

## Narratively Intelligent
- [ ] At least one case study or vignette anchors the chapter.
- [ ] Stories illuminate mechanisms; they do not substitute for evidence.

## Philosophically Reflective
- [ ] At least one clarifying question sharpens a definition (e.g., "What is autonomy?").
- [ ] Questions do not replace scientific claims.

## Anti-Deterministic
- [ ] Development is framed as probabilistic, not destiny.
- [ ] No parental-fate framing ("if you miss X, your child will never Y").

If any box is unchecked, revise before committing.
