# Early Physical Reasoning Is Not a Finished Physics

> Domain: early learning
> Last updated: 2026-09-16
> Status: Source-audited first pass (CH06 causal representation)

## What We Know

Infants look longer at some events that violate physical expectations (for example, a solid object appearing to pass through another). Baillargeon (1987) is the classic looking-time demonstration that young infants are sensitive to occlusion events adults find impossible. Stahl & Feigenson (2015) show that such a mismatch can also reorganize later exploration. A useful sequence — regularity, prediction, contingency, intervention, causal representation — should not be collapsed into one word.

## How Confident We Are

**Grade**: Moderate that violation-of-expectation looking indexes some physical expectations in specified tasks, and that those mismatches can guide exploration. **Low** that looking or reaching in those tasks is already an articulate “why,” a complete object concept, or adult-like causal theory.

## Evidence and Causal Assessment

Laboratory looking-time and follow-on exploration. The linking hypotheses from “looked longer” to “represented impossibility” remain a live methods debate (`looking-time-as-measurement.md`). Search tasks and looking tasks can dissociate (`object-permanence-looking-vs-search.md`). Contingency learning (`infant-contingency-learning.md`) sits earlier in the sequence (action–outcome), not at mature cause.

## Effect Size and Persistence

No single standardized “physics” effect. Persistence of a particular expectation into later explanation is largely untested on this card.

## Bias, Heterogeneity, and Confounding

GI attenuation: **n/a**. Perceptual novelty, timing, and rich versus lean interpretation of looking are the standing threats.

## What Remains Uncertain

How laboratory physical expectations relate to everyday problem solving, and when intervention (doing something to make an outcome happen) becomes distinct from noticing a regularity.

## What This Does Not Mean

Does not mean infants are miniature physicists. Does not mean a looking-time difference is an explanation. Does not mean failing a search task is absence of a mind. Does not license toys that “teach cause.”

## What Action Is Reasonably Justified

No parenting protocol follows. Keep looking-time claims matched to the measurement.
