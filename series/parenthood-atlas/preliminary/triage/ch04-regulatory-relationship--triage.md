## 2. Developmental window

Newborn through roughly the first year: co-regulation of arousal, feeding, sleep, temperature, attention, and distress. Attachment is one organization of this relationship, not a synonym for good parenting.

## 6. Prohibited inferences

- The Strange Situation is not a personality test.
- Sensitivity is not a script for destiny.
- Responding to crying is not automatically “spoiling,” and waiting is not automatically “teaching independence.”
- Severe institutional deprivation is not ordinary parenting variation.
- Nurturing care is an ecology (health, nutrition, safety, learning), not a maternal purity test.
