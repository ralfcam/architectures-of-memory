## 6. Prohibited inferences

- Regulation ≠ suppression, obedience, or constant calm
- Difficult temperament ≠ poor parenting or poor outcome
- Tantrums ≠ manipulation or relational failure
- Behavioral inhibition ≠ diagnosis or fixed future
- Stranger wariness ≠ insecure attachment (already a Chapter 8 prohibition)
- Still-face protest ≠ attachment classification
- Resilience ≠ invulnerability
- Parenting quality alone does not explain emotional development
- Preschool intervention effects do not transfer unchanged to infants
