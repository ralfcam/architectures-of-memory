## 2. Developmental window

Toddler through age 3: emerging inhibitory control, working memory, flexibility, and goal maintenance. Early behaviors are precursors, not miniature adult executive competence. Performance remains state- and context-dependent.

## 6. Prohibited inferences

- EF ≠ intelligence, academic worth, or moral character
- A low score ≠ poor parenting or a fixed deficit
- A delay-task result does not determine future achievement
- Immature prefrontal cortex is not a sufficient explanation of tantrums
- A short program does not reliably create durable self-control
- A single task is not a general executive capacity
- Emotion cannot be separated from everyday control
- Early differences necessarily persist
- Parent advice cannot substitute for institutional support when sleep, safety, time, or childcare are constrained
