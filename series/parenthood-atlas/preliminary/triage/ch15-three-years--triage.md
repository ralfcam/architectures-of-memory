## 6. Prohibited inferences

- Age three ≠ adulthood as destiny
- Early adversity ≠ a fixed outcome
- Early advantage ≠ guaranteed later success
- Parenting alone does not explain the waypoint
- Biology does not write an irreversible script
- Plasticity does not mean everything can be undone
- Resilience does not mean adversity is harmless
- Prediction ≠ individual forecast
- Intervention efficacy ≠ inequality eliminated
- Do not treat Dunedin self-control as a marshmallow-test replication
- Do not treat BEIP effect sizes as ordinary three-year-old catch-up
