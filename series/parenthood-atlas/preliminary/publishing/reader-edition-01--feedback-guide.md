# Reader feedback guide

Thank you for reading a private working manuscript. This is a qualitative usability pass, not a survey and not a vote on the science. Use a reader code rather than your name in repository-bound notes.

## Two useful feedback modes

| Mode | Best for | How |
|---|---|---|
| Margin notes | Local confusion, pacing, awkward wording, emotional reactions | Mark the printed copy or annotate the PDF; include the page |
| End-of-book response | Cross-chapter patterns and overall experience | Answer any of the questions below in about 15–20 minutes |

## Questions

1. Where did you lose the thread?
2. Which chapter or passage felt clearest, most useful, or most memorable?
3. Which passage felt overly technical, repetitive, alarming, preachy, abstract, or vague?
4. Did you understand the difference between a High finding and a recommended action? Where did that distinction blur?
5. Did any passage sound more deterministic than the evidence appeared to warrant?
6. Did any passage make parents seem individually responsible for institutional, material, or social conditions?
7. Did the Science / Interpretation / Art framework become clearer as you read? Where did it not?
8. Which parts would you return to during pregnancy, infancy, or toddlerhood?
9. Which sections felt too long or too short?
10. Which terms need a plain-language definition?
11. Were chapter transitions and section breaks easy to follow?
12. Did the “What this does not mean” framing feel integrated, repetitive, or defensive?
13. Were evidence-card filenames understandable as references, even if you would never open them?
14. What, if anything, made you trust the book more or less?
15. What is the one editorial change that would most improve the reading experience?

## Please do not adjudicate

Do not decide whether a causal claim is valid, a card should become `stable`, a citation is correctly interpreted, or a clinical recommendation is safe. Flag the concern and its location; those questions require evidence review.

## Recording a response

For each comment, record:

- Edition: `Reader Edition 0.1`
- Reader code
- Chapter and printed page
- Type: clarity, pacing, tone, overreach, structure, usefulness, typo, or verify evidence
- Your words, without trying to rewrite the passage

Feedback is requested by 2026-10-16. Reading selectively is useful and explicitly permitted.
