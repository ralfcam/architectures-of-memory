# Evidence and uncertainty

The evidence cards, not this PDF, are the project’s unit of scientific record. Reader Edition 0.1 freezes the prose of fifteen curated chapters at one commit. It does not create new claims or change the catalog: 68 cards remain live, with 7 `stable` for narrow claims and 61 `draft`.

Grades travel with claims, not topics. A High laboratory or prevention finding may sit beside Low confidence in household transfer, later outcomes, diagnosis, or prescription. Association, prediction, contribution, causation, prevention, and determinism are not interchangeable.

The book’s boundaries matter as much as its positive statements. Biological relevance is not parental control. Modifiability is not individual responsibility. Family action is not a substitute for institutional obligation.

Private reader feedback tests whether those distinctions are understandable and usable. It is not scientific validation. Agreement cannot change a grade, and discomfort does not make an accurate claim false. But confusion, alarm, or a deterministic reading may show that technically accurate prose needs context, a clearer boundary, or a better explanation.

Mark apparent factual errors or unsupported recommendations as **verify evidence**. They will be investigated through the evidence-card and extraction workflow before any manuscript revision.
