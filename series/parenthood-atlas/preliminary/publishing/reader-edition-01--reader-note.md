# Private-circulation reader note

This is a working manuscript for private feedback. It is a book about what developmental science can and cannot support from before conception through age three. It is not individualized medical, mental-health, legal, or parenting advice.

Its central discipline is to separate **science**, **interpretation**, and **art**: a research finding does not automatically become a prescription for families.

Please respond as a reader, not as a scientific reviewer. The useful questions are where the argument becomes unclear, technical, repetitive, alarming, preachy, abstract, or vague; where the pacing drags; where the tone earns or loses trust; and where a boundary against overclaiming does or does not work.

Reader Edition 0.1 is dated 2026-09-18. Please attach the edition label and printed page number to every comment. Feedback is requested by 2026-10-16. Selective reading is welcome; completing all fifteen chapters is not a condition of helping.

# How to read this book

- Read in sequence or choose the periods most relevant to you. The chapters form an arc, but each should carry its own claim boundaries.
- A **High** grade belongs to a specific claim. It does not certify an entire theory, guarantee an outcome, or automatically justify an action.
- **Science** asks what can be observed and reasonably inferred. **Interpretation** asks what that evidence means and where it stops. **Art** includes judgment, relationship, culture, individuality, uncertainty, and lived experience.
- “What this does not mean” sections are part of the argument, not optional disclaimers.
- Filenames such as `infant-caregiver-coregulation.md` point to the project’s evidence record. Readers do not need GitHub or those files to read this edition.
- Margin notes are best for local reactions. The guide at the back is for patterns across chapters.

If a passage appears factually wrong, mark it for verification. Do not try to settle the evidence question in the margin; it will be routed back through the research workflow.
