## 1. Executive Summary

Reader feedback across both complete manuscript copies affirmed the clarity and integrity of the **Science $\to$ Interpretation $\to$ Art** architecture and the OKF v0.2 certainty grading framework. Readers specifically highlighted that the book eliminates parental anxiety without slipping into nihilism.

Five concrete editorial improvement areas were identified during the reading pass, all of which have been systematically implemented and closed across Passes 1.1–1.4 and 2.1:
1. **Methodological Bounding of Looking Time:** Prevent conflating infant novelty preference with rich adult conceptual representation (`ARC-52`).
2. **Pedagogical Separation of Guided Play:** Distinguish between unstructured pretend play (which has weak academic transfer) and teacher/caregiver guided play (which has strong trial support) (`ARC-50`).
3. **Causal Clarification of Delivery Mode:** Disambiguate immediate respiratory transition physiology from confounded observational links to long-term neurodevelopment (`ARC-50`).
4. **Chapter 2 Depth & Physical Realism:** Expand placental transport, acoustic low-pass filtering, and transient neural architectures to bring Chapter 2 to full chapter weight (expanded to 2,815 words) (`ARC-60`).
5. **Systematic GI Confounding Audits:** Backfill 100% of observational evidence cards with explicit genetically-informed attenuation ratings to protect against deterministic over-interpretation (`ARC-51`).
