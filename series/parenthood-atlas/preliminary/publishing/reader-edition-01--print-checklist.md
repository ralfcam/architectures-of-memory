## Copy A — reading proof

US Letter, double-sided, generous margins, annotation-friendly binding or staple.

- [ ] Body text is comfortable at reading distance.
- [ ] Inner and outer margins leave room for notes.
- [ ] Headers, footers, page numbers, lists, and tables are legible.
- [ ] Every chapter begins on a new page.
- [ ] Contents entries match printed chapter-start pages.
- [ ] No blank, clipped, duplicated, or missing pages.
- [ ] Widows, orphans, bad section splits, and cramped tables are marked.
- [ ] Grayscale contrast is sufficient.
- [ ] Evidence-card filenames are understandable to a non-GitHub reader.
- [ ] “What this does not mean” passages feel integrated rather than appended defensively.
