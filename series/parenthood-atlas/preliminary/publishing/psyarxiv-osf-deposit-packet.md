## 2. Abstract (99 Words)

> This chapter examines the biological and conceptual environment of human development prior to birth, focusing on periconceptional folate, maternal-fetal endocrine mediation via placental 11β-HSD2, and the teratological continuum of prenatal alcohol and substance exposure. Governed by the Open Knowledge Format (OKF v0.2), we distinguish empirical observations from clinical interpretations and prescriptive parenting mandates. Observational associations regarding typical-range prenatal maternal stress largely attenuate under genetically informed and sibling-comparison designs, while neural-tube defect prevention via periconceptional folic acid represents a narrow, robust causal intervention. The prenatal environment functions as a dynamic physiological filter rather than an indelible personality forecast.
