### CH09-C01 — Caregivers and infants dynamically regulate one another

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Claim | Adult support is an important context for infant regulation; the process is bidirectional. The exact claim that repeated co-regulation *becomes* internal self-regulation is a model, not a demonstrated causal law. |
| Sources | Already on `infant-caregiver-coregulation.md` (Feldman 2012; WHO nurturing-care framework). |
| Label | **Reconcile only** — existing card. |
| Grade | Moderate existence; Low-to-Moderate mechanism (unchanged). |

### CH09-C01b — Still-face disruption of expected contingency

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Claim | When a familiar adult suddenly becomes unresponsive, infants typically protest, look away, and try to re-engage. This is a laboratory signature of expected contingency, not an attachment diagnosis. |
| Sources | Tronick et al. 1978 *JAACP* DOI 10.1016/S0002-7138(09)62273-1; Mesman, van IJzendoorn & Bakermans-Kranenburg 2009 *Developmental Review* DOI 10.1016/j.dr.2009.02.001. |
| Label | `promote` — card `evidence-cards/infant-still-face-paradigm.md` |
| Grade | High laboratory procedure effect; Low as attachment/parenting verdict. |

### CH09-C03 — Temperament contributes to variation in reactivity and control

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Claim | Early-emerging dimensional differences (surgency, negative affectivity, regulatory capacity) are measurable and moderately stable; they are not destiny or a parenting score. |
| Sources | Gartstein & Rothbart 2003 *Infant Behavior and Development* DOI 10.1016/S0163-6383(02)00169-8. |
| Label | `promote` — card `evidence-cards/temperament-reactivity-not-destiny.md` |
| Grade | Moderate differences; Low destiny. |

### CH09-C06 — Fear and inhibition probabilistically predict anxiety risk

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Claim | Behavioral inhibition is a group-level risk marker for later social anxiety; it is neither necessary nor sufficient. Ordinary stranger wariness is not this claim. |
| Sources | Clauss & Blackford 2012 *JAACAP* DOI 10.1016/j.jaac.2012.08.002. |
| Label | `promote` — card `evidence-cards/behavioral-inhibition-anxiety-risk.md` |
| Grade | Moderate group risk; Low individual forecast. |

## 3. Prohibited inferences

- Still-face protest ≠ insecure attachment
- Difficult temperament ≠ poor parenting
- Inhibition ≠ diagnosis
- Tantrums ≠ manipulation
- Regulation ≠ suppression
- Expression ≠ weakness
- Parenting quality alone does not explain emotional development
