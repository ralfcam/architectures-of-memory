| Field | Content |

## 3. Prohibited inferences

- Play ≠ a manufactured intelligence program
- Pretend ≠ guaranteed ToM/EF/IQ
- More toys ≠ more information
- Limited pretense ≠ pathology
- Guided play ≠ universally superior
- Perry/Abecedarian ≠ “play causes IQ”
